Reano Room

Home Design & Interior Visualization App

Reano is a mobile application that helps renters and homeowners confidently redesign their living spaces before making expensive furniture and paint purchases. Through mood boards, AR visualization, room planning, and personalized inspiration, the app simplifies the home design experience.

Role UI/UX Designer
Tools Figma · Miro
Project Type Mobile Application
Year 2026
View Figma
— Overview

Designing confidence into every home redesign.

Reano was created to help renters and homeowners visualize their spaces before committing to a purchase. Furniture, paint and decor are expensive and hard to return — and most people are left guessing how a piece will actually look in their own room.

The app brings inspiration, planning and visualization into one intuitive experience: mood boards for gathering ideas, room tools for laying things out, and AR for previewing furniture life-size, in place, before it's bought.

— Early Exploration

Wireframes

Early low fidelity wireframes were used to test structure before visual design. The focus stayed narrow on purpose — simplifying room planning, furniture browsing and visualization — so the layout could be validated without the noise of finished UI.

— The Problem

The Challenge

Many homeowners struggle to imagine how furniture, colours and layouts will look in their own space before purchasing. Existing tools are fragmented, making inspiration, planning and visualization difficult to bring together.

Difficult to visualize how furniture will actually look in a real room.

Colour choices are uncertain and rarely match how they'll look at home.

Inspiration is scattered across too many disconnected platforms.

Expensive purchases end up involving avoidable guesswork.

— The Solution

A complete room planning experience.

Mood Boards

Create and organize inspiration collections in one place, pulled from anywhere and kept together by room.

AR Furniture Placement

Preview furniture directly inside a real room, at true scale, before any purchase is made.

Wall Colour Preview

Experiment with paint colours on real walls before committing to a can and a weekend.

Saved Designs & Wishlist

Store favourite layouts and products for later, so ideas don't get lost between visits.

— Key Screens

A closer look at the experience.

01Home Dashboard

The dashboard gives users quick access to their active projects, saved rooms and daily inspiration — so returning to an in-progress redesign never means starting from scratch.

02Room Visualization

Users explore layouts, wall colours and furniture placement side by side, comparing options before settling on a direction for the room.

03Mood Boards

Inspiration is collected and organized into boards by room or theme, giving scattered ideas a single, structured home.

04AR Experience

Users preview furniture using augmented reality, placing pieces directly in their room at true scale before making a purchase.
