Introduction

A little about me

Hello! I’m Jaskirat Singh, a UI/UX designer and web development student at Langara College in Vancouver, Canada. I enjoy creating digital products that are clean, user friendly, and designed to solve real-world problems.

I completed a Bachelor of Science in India before moving to Canada to study Web and Mobile App Design and Development. During my studies, I have worked on UX research, wireframing, prototyping, responsive web design, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern design tools such as Figma.

My goal is to create digital experiences that are visually appealing, practical, and easy to use. I enjoy learning new technologies, collaborating with others, and continuously improving my design and development skills.
